INCENTIVES

Explore the support available.

The playbook describes 5% corporate tax for up to 15 years for qualifying manufacturing activities in flagship zones, for companies investing at least RM500 million, excluding land. It also describes grants and other incentives.

Playbook, p. 6 ↗
↗

TAX WATCH-OUTS

Consider how you operate across borders.

Cross-border activities may create a taxable presence. Related-party transactions should follow the arm's length principle and be supported by records.

Playbook, p. 14 ↗
◎

PASSES FOR STAFF

Match the pass to the work.

The playbook discusses Employment Passes and Professional Visit Passes. The latter covers temporary professional work for an overseas company, for up to 12 months, and is not extendable.

Playbook, appendix p. 28 ↗

Requirements can change. Consult the playbook and relevant authorities when planning your expansion.
